RRL Hub

Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

RRL Hub was made by Justin M. Bardiago to help students find their Review of Related Literature (RRL). This policy explains what information RRL Hub collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account information you provide. If you create an account, you provide your name, email address, a password, and a profile photo.
  • Google sign-in information. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, and profile photo with RRL Hub so your account can be created.
  • Your activity in the app. The sites you mark as favorites and the topics you search for are saved so they can be shown on your dashboard.

Where your information is stored

Your account, profile photo, favorites, and searches are stored only in your own web browser, on your own device (browser local storage). RRL Hub has no server for accounts and does not send your account information to the developer or to anyone else. If you use RRL Hub on another device or clear your browser data, your account and saved items on that device are gone.

Reviews you post (your name, profile photo, star rating, and comment) are stored in RRL Hub’s shared database (Firebase, Google) so that other users can read them. Anything you write in a review is public — do not include private information in a review.

Online status: while you are signed in and using RRL Hub, your name and profile photo appear in the “Active now” list on the dashboard so other signed-in users can see who is online. Your status is removed when you sign out, close the app, or go offline (after a few minutes without activity).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share, or rent your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not show advertisements and we do not use third-party tracking or analytics.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, or files.

External links

RRL Hub links to research sites such as Google Scholar, ResearchGate, ScienceDirect, and others. When you tap a site, you leave RRL Hub and that site’s own privacy policy applies. RRL Hub is not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Children’s privacy

RRL Hub is intended for students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information through RRL Hub, contact us and we will help remove it from the device.

Removing your information

Because your information lives only in your browser, you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for RRL Hub, or by logging out and clearing your browser data. There is no server copy to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
