About RRL Hub

RRL Hub was made by Justin M. Bardiago to help students, researchers, teachers, and anyone who needs reliable academic sources find what they are looking for — faster and easier.

Whether you are writing a thesis, preparing a lesson, working on a research project, or simply checking a fact, RRL Hub puts the most useful research search sites in one place. Type your topic once, tap any site, and you go straight to the search results for that topic.

What you can do here

Search 12 research sites from one page: Google Scholar, ResearchGate, ScienceDirect, Semantic Scholar, DOAJ, CORE, BASE, ERIC, IEEE Xplore, SpringerLink, JSTOR, and PLOS ONE.

Sign in with your Google account, or create an account with your email, to save your favorite sites, keep your recent searches on your dashboard, and rate RRL Hub.

Install the app

Get RRL Hub on your device, just like any other app. On Windows, open this page in Chrome or Microsoft Edge, then click the install icon in the address bar (or open the menu and choose “Install RRL Hub”). On Android, use Chrome and tap Install. On iPhone or iPad, tap Share and choose “Add to Home Screen”.

Privacy

Your account, favorites, and searches are stored only on your device. Reviews you post (your name, photo, rating, and comment) are shared with other users so everyone can read them. Read our Privacy Policy.

Note

RRL Hub is free to use. The research sites linked here belong to their respective owners; RRL Hub only links to them. Always cite the sources you use.
